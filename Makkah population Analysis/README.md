# Makkah population Analysis
##The provided dataset offers a comprehensive insight into the demographic dynamics
of the Makkah region over a span of thirteen years, from 2010 to 2022. It 
meticulously categorizes the population estimates based on gender, nationality 
(Saudi and Non-Saudi), and year. This data showcases the evolving composition of 
the population in Makkah, revealing fluctuations in male and female populations as 
well as the presence of Saudi and Non-Saudi residents. Such information is essential 
for policymakers, researchers, and businesses looking to understand the region's 
demographic trends, plan infrastructure, and develop services that cater to the 
specific needs of these diverse populations. This dataset offers a valuable resource 
for a deeper exploration of Makkah's demographic landscape and its implications for 
the region's development.
